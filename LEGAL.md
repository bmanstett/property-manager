# California lease preparation

Installed research package **CA-2026.11.18.2**, reviewed November 18, 2026. The full offline source register appears in **Leases → View rules & updates** and in `rules.js`. Each entry records the rule ID, jurisdiction, section, source links, dates if established, facts and review status. The software produces an English-language residential draft. The signing packet uses the completed agreement reviewed for that tenancy. The only city-specific rule set installed is the City of San Diego. Confirm the parcel jurisdiction even when the mailing address says San Diego.

## Preparing an agreement

1. Choose **Create lease** and link the household. Enter the verified parcel jurisdiction and source, the owner and title structure, original occupancy and tenancy dates. A postal city can differ from the legal jurisdiction.
2. List the authorized landlord signer and each adult tenant separately. Record agreed terms, property responsibilities and the disclosure facts you know. In **Terms → Additional clauses**, choose **Add clause** for each provision. Enter its wording and an optional title. Use the move controls to set the order, then **Save & close** or **Save & continue**. The live preview, draft PDF and printable HTML show the clauses in that order.
3. Review each legal outcome. **More information needed** identifies missing inputs. **Review needed** identifies judgments or exceptions requiring case-specific review. **Required** identifies a triggered action. **Not applicable** records a condition outside the rule. Keep any official notice, translation, exemption documentation and proof of delivery with its own version in Documents.
4. **Generate draft packet** preserves a selectable-text PDF, printable HTML, rule review and selected exhibits. The generator includes tenant-facing notices according to the inputs and package. Bracketed text identifies missing information. The packet includes the English CDPH mold booklet for a qualifying new lease, English EPA materials for a required federal lead disclosure and the English SDHC guide for covered city housing. Complete the property-specific forms, obtain the language versions needed and retain evidence of timely delivery. A file added after signing cannot establish pre-signing delivery.
5. For a signing packet, attach the completed, tenancy-specific attorney-reviewed agreement PDF, reviewer, date, scope and authority to use it. Incorporate each additional clause into that PDF or a reviewed exhibit before freezing. Identify the page, clause or exhibit for each custom provision, then record the disposition of each triggered legal item. **Freeze reviewed packet** preserves the supplied PDF and exhibits exactly. The app records those references for the owner. It cannot compare their legal meaning with the reviewed PDF. When lead disclosure applies, include the completed lead disclosure and available reports. The blank EPA form is supplied in draft packets only.
6. Download the ZIP, print the reviewed document for signing, or prepare an ordinary email. Import each returned signed copy and any provider certificate; select the actual signers verified on those pages. The app tracks complete signer evidence separately from designation of a complete operative copy. When pages were returned separately, import the assembled complete copy in Documents before designation. An amendment starts a new draft linked to the earlier immutable packet. Email and signing actions do not document statutory service.

## Update a rule package

Export JSON in **Leases → View rules & updates → Install reviewed update**. Start from that file, assign a distinct version, identify the verification date and describe material changes. Recheck each affected original section, chapter, effective/sunset date, municipal boundary and agency publication. Retain consultation notes outside the package. Use the existing declarative trigger names and fact identifiers. The import validates the package format, record count, stable IDs, supported jurisdictions, source hostnames, date values and enumerated conditions. The format cannot run JavaScript. New calculations and new jurisdictions require a reviewed software release.

Import the JSON, inspect the source changes and resulting differences for drafts, and record the reviewer's identity and evidence before installation. Source review is a human step. Validation cannot authenticate the publisher or verify the law. For a draft pinned to an older package missing current topics, use **Packet → Update draft rules** and recheck the answers. Updating an editable draft preserves its inputs. Previously generated packets and signed documents retain their original bytes.
